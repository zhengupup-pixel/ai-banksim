# Product Context

AI BankSim is for bank teller practical training.

Primary users:

- Students practicing teller workflows
- Teachers creating scenarios and reviewing progress
- Competition/demo judges evaluating innovation and technical completeness

Core value:

- Realistic scenarios
- Deterministic business compliance checking
- AI customer simulation and coaching
- Personalized improvement loop

The project should emphasize credible business process design and a complete training experience over superficial AI chat.
